BETA PRIVACY NOTICE

Draft for the private beta · Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Version beta-2026-11

This notice covers the invite-only test version of the Aevigor app at aevigor.com/beta. It adds to our privacy policy. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). Some of the data below is health information, so we ask for your clear consent before you use the app.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your name if you give it or sign in with Google, your time zone, and the date you agreed to this notice.
  • From intervals.icu (only after you connect it): heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep time, the wellness answers stored there, and the date, sport and training load of your recent and planned workouts.
  • Daily check-in: sleep quality, fatigue, muscle soreness, stress, mood, motivation, whether you feel sick (none, above the neck, below the neck) and any notes you type.
  • Your intervals.icu API key, encrypted before it is stored. It is used only to read your data and to copy your check-in into your intervals.icu wellness log.
  • Results we calculate: your daily status, weekly check and the signal values behind them.

2. Why we use it

Only to work out and show you your daily training status, to keep your account working and secure, and to fix problems you report. We use the least data needed for this.

3. What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share your information for advertising or marketing. We do not combine testers' data or show it to other testers.

4. Who can see it

  • You: the database only returns your own rows to your account.
  • Ben Wiseman, who runs Aevigor: as the administrator he can technically view stored data in the database. He will only look when needed to fix a problem or answer a request from you.
  • Service providers that store or process data for us: Supabase (accounts and database, hosted in Canada), GitHub and Cloudflare (website hosting and network), Resend (account emails such as confirmation and password reset), Google (only if you choose Sign in with Google) and intervals.icu (where your data comes from). Some providers may process limited data, such as emails or logs, outside Canada, where foreign laws may apply.

5. Your choices

  • You can disconnect intervals.icu at any time in Settings. Syncing stops and your key is deleted.
  • You can delete your account in Settings. This permanently deletes your account, check-ins, synced data, results and key from our database right away. Backups held by our database provider are removed on their normal schedule.
  • You can ask to see or correct your information, or withdraw consent, by emailing bjwiseman@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

6. How long we keep it

While your beta account is open. When the beta ends we will tell you before anything changes, and ask for consent again before moving your data to a paid or public version.

7. Security

Encrypted connections, encrypted API keys, per-person access rules in the database, and invite-only sign-up. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the regulators as the law requires.

8. Not medical advice

Aevigor gives training guidance only. See the medical disclaimer.

9. Contact and complaints

bjwiseman@gmail.com (Attention: Privacy). You can also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
