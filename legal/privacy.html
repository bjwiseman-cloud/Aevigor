PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Aevigor respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your choices. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. What we collect

  • Waitlist: your name, email address, the date and time you signed up, and your consent to be emailed.
  • App (private testing): training and wellness data you choose to connect or enter, such as workouts, power, heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and daily check-ins. This is personal health information.
  • Future features: if you use supplement, medication or medical-record features, we will collect that information only with your clear, separate consent.
  • Website: our website does not use advertising, tracking cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security.

2. Why we use it

To run the waitlist and tell you about early access; to provide the app's features, such as your daily status, charts and reminders; to keep the Service secure; and to meet legal obligations. We use only what we need for these purposes.

3. What we never do

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not use your health information for advertising.

4. Who handles it

We use trusted service providers to store and process information on our behalf, including Google (Google Sheets, Apps Script and email), GitHub (website hosting) and Cloudflare (domain and network services), and, if you connect it, intervals.icu. Some of these providers store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessed by authorities under local law. We share information only as needed to run the Service, when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.

5. Consent

By joining the waitlist you consent to us emailing you about Aevigor. Every email will include a way to unsubscribe, and you can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. You can also withdraw consent for the app to use your data; some features will then stop working.

6. How long we keep it

Waitlist details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or until the waitlist ends. App data is kept while you use the app and deleted on request, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as access controls and encrypted connections, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the regulators as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

The Service is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Contact and complaints

Questions or requests: bjwiseman@gmail.com (Attention: Privacy). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
