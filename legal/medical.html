TALK TO A PROFESSIONAL FIRST

Talk to your doctor or another qualified health professional before you start a new exercise program, change how hard or how much you train, or start, stop or change any supplement or medication. This matters even more if you have a heart, lung, blood pressure, metabolic or other medical condition, are pregnant, take prescription medication, or have not exercised in a while.

Never ignore professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you read or saw in Aevigor.

STOP AND GET HELP

Stop exercising and seek medical help right away if you have chest pain or pressure, severe shortness of breath, fainting or near-fainting, dizziness, an irregular or racing heartbeat, or any other worrying symptom. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

ABOUT THE NUMBERS

Aevigor uses data from consumer devices and services, such as watches, heart rate straps, power meters and intervals.icu, and from what you enter yourself. These measurements can be wrong, missing or delayed. Training statuses such as "Go", "Caution" or "Go easy" are general guidance based on published research and coaching practice. They are not a medical judgment about your health or your fitness to exercise.

Research summaries on our Research page describe published studies, which often involved trained or professional athletes. Results may not apply to you.

SUPPLEMENTS, MEDICATION AND MEDICAL RECORDS

When these features are released, they will help you log, organise and get reminders about what you take and your appointments, and see trends next to your training. Any analysis of medical results will be educational only; review your results with your doctor.
