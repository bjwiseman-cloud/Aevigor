◢ WHAT IT DOES

🚦Daily status

Go, OK, Caution or Go easy, from how you feel, your HRV trend and your sleep, each compared with your own normal.

📈Training load

Fitness, fatigue and form per sport, planned vs actual load, and a countdown to your next test.

🏋️Longevity training

Cycling, strength and powerlifting, and racket sports: chosen to build fitness that lasts, with less impact.

⚡Performance

Power curve, FTP and VO2 tests, aerobic efficiency, decoupling and durability after 1500 kJ.

◢ BUILT ON EVIDENCE

  • HRV-guided training with a 7-day rolling average against a personal baseline (Plews et al. 2013).
  • How you feel is weighted most: self-reported wellness tracks training stress better than objective markers (Saw et al. 2016).
  • Durability: how much power you keep late in a long ride (Maunder et al. 2021).
