◢ WHAT IT DOES

🚦Daily status

Go, OK, Caution or Go easy, from how you feel, your HRV trend and your sleep, each compared with your own normal.

📈Training load

Monitor, track and analyse your load: fitness, fatigue and form per sport, planned vs actual load, and a countdown to your next test, race or event.

🌱Built for longevity

Everything in Aevigor is designed around healthspan: training that keeps you strong, fit and moving well for decades. Cycling, running, strength training and racket sports, balanced to build fitness that lasts.

⚡Performance

Monitor, track and analyse performance in every sport you train:

  • Cycling: power curve, FTP and VO2 tests, aerobic efficiency, decoupling and durability after 1500 kJ.
  • Running: pace, threshold pace, heart rate vs pace efficiency and decoupling.
  • Strength: estimated 1-rep maxes for powerlifting, volume load (sets × reps × weight) for bodybuilding, and reps in reserve.
  • Badminton: session load (effort × minutes), time on court and heart rate during play.

◢ BUILT ON EVIDENCE

All metrics and recommendations are based on peer-reviewed scientific literature. Where research doesn't exist yet, we use established coaching best practice, and we say so.

  • Readiness: how you feel is weighted most, because self-reported wellness tracks training stress better than objective markers. HRV and resting heart rate are compared with your own 60-day normal using a 7-day trend.
  • Recovery: sleep, sickness and overreaching signals follow sports-science consensus.
  • Training load: fitness, fatigue and form use the impulse-response model, with session effort for lifting and racket sports.
  • Performance: power profiling, aerobic efficiency, cardiovascular drift and durability research in cycling, plus reps in reserve and volume research in strength training.
  • Longevity: research linking fitness, strength, cycling and racket sports with a longer, healthier life. These studies show links, not proof that any app or plan will extend your life.
